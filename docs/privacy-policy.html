Privacy Policy for Life Metric

Effective date: October 28, 2026
App: Life Metric (com.markbriz.lifemetric)

This Privacy Policy explains how Life Metric handles information on your device. Life Metric is a personal fitness and health tracking app. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

1. Summary

Life Metric stores the data you enter on this device only. The app does not create an account, does not sign you in, and does not send your information to a server. There is no cloud sync, advertising, or analytics SDK in the app at the time of this policy.

2. Information stored on your device

You may enter and store:

  • Profile: first name, last name, height, waist, theme, display units, and optional goals (weight target and starting reading, blood pressure targets, moving-average window)
  • Weight readings: date, time, weight, optional tags and notes
  • Blood pressure readings: date, time, systolic, diastolic, optional heart rate, tags, notes, and pairing metadata you create in the app
  • Workout plans and activities: plan names, activity names, and descriptions
  • Workout sessions: date, plan, duration, notes, and set details (load, reps, distance, time, intervals, resistance, speed, cadence, heart rate, set kind, RPE, rest)

The app also stores:

  • Your acceptance of the in-app health disclaimer
  • Optional reminder on/off state and reminder times
  • An optional rest-timer setting for workouts

This information is user-entered. Life Metric does not read Health Connect, Google Fit, a scale, a cuff, GPS, or other sensors.

3. Permissions

The app may request:

PermissionWhy
Notifications Optional morning or evening reminders to log weight, blood pressure, or a workout. Reminders stay off unless you turn them on.
Run at startup Restore those reminders after the device reboots.

The app does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or health-platform access.

4. How information is used

Information on the device is used only to:

  • Show your profile, history, charts, and in-app insights (for example BMI from height and weight you provide)
  • Prefill recent values when you log another set or reading
  • Schedule the reminders you enable
  • Build an export file or a shareable report when you ask for one

We do not sell your personal information.
We do not use your health data for advertising.

5. Where information is stored

Tracking data is saved in a local file on your device (life-metric-local.json in the app’s private storage). That file is excluded from Android cloud backup and from automatic device-to-device transfer.

Copies leave the device only when you act:

  • Export data writes a JSON file and opens the system share sheet so you can save or send it
  • Share report / share log writes a text and CSV file and opens the share sheet
  • Load data reads a JSON file you pick and replaces the tracking data on this device

Anyone you send those files to can read them. Choose destinations you trust.

6. How information is shared

Life Metric does not upload your data and does not share it with advertisers, analytics services, or data brokers.

The Android share sheet may send a file you export to an app you select (for example Files, email, or messaging). Those apps have their own policies.

7. Data retention

  • Entries stay on the device until you delete them in the app, use Erase all data, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.
  • Uninstalling removes local app storage. It does not delete copies you already exported or shared.
  • Loading a JSON file replaces the tracking data currently on the device.

8. Your choices

You can:

  • Edit or delete individual readings, workouts, plans, and profile fields in the app
  • Turn reminders off
  • Export or share your data
  • Replace on-device data by loading a JSON file
  • Erase all tracking data from Profile → Data
  • Uninstall the app to remove local storage

9. Children’s privacy

Life Metric is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Security

Data stays in the app’s private storage on your device. Protect access to the device the same way you would any other personal health notes. No method of storage is 100% secure. Exported or shared files are not encrypted by Life Metric.

11. Health disclaimer

Life Metric provides personal tracking tools only. It does not provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical questions. Do not ignore professional medical advice because of something you see in the app.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above will be revised when that happens. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
