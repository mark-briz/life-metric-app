BP

Blood pressure

Log systolic, diastolic, and optional heart rate. See the latest reading, a trend chart, AHA category labels, and how often you stay at or under the target your clinician gave you.

lb

Weight

Record scale readings, watch a 7-day trend, and follow a goal you set. BMI and waist are optional body measures, calculated from height you enter.

W

Workouts

Build a plan, then log the session. Strength sets, cardio time, rest timer, and a week view against the familiar 150-minute / 2-strength-day adult activity target.

One dashboard. Three logs.

Open the app and see the last blood pressure, the weight trend, and the last workout. Each area has the same layout: latest value, trend, goal, then history.

  • Reminders on this phone, if you want them — morning or evening.
  • Export or load a JSON backup from Profile. You stay in control of the file.
  • Four color themes, including Dark.

Your numbers stay on this phone.

Life Metric does not create an account, does not sign you in, and does not upload your readings to a server. Data is stored only in the app’s private storage and is protected by your device lock. If you uninstall the app without exporting, that data is gone.
